About the Project

Our model was trained to distinguish between three common kitchen objects: Cup, Bowl, and Plate. We used original images and tested how well the model could identify each object through a live webcam feed.

This project also connects to themes from Unmasking AI, especially the importance of training data, representation, and the limits of machine learning systems when data is narrow or incomplete.
